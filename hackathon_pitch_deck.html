Slide 1: Title Slide

AyuSync AI

Unified Longitudinal Health & Care Orchestration Platform

Your medical context travels with you. No lost files, no repeated tests, no claim delays.

Executive Summary:
AyuSync AI is not another document locker. While standard apps (like DigiLocker or ABHA portals) simply store files, AyuSync actively parses, structures, and utilizes medical data. It compiles scattered histories into a clean, printable Visit Summary Report for doctors, coordinates emergency hospital transfers in advance, and automates insurance clearances in the background.

Slide 2: Problem Description

The Clutter and "Guesswork" in Indian Healthcare

Healthcare records in India are highly fragmented. Patients navigate between separate private clinics, government hospitals, labs, and pharmacies. Because there is no unified digital transport system, patients are expected to carry their own history—resulting in lost files, repeat test costs, and clinical guesswork during emergencies.

  • The Emergency Referral Gap: In critical emergency transfers (e.g., from a local clinic to a major hospital like AIIMS), families leave thick paper files behind in the rush. Doctors must treat the patient with zero history, guessing drug allergies and active prescriptions under extreme time pressure.
  • The "WhatsApp Black Hole": Over 70% of outpatient records (prescriptions, blood reports) are shared as loose photos on WhatsApp. These documents quickly get buried in chat histories and are never organized or digitized.
  • Duplicate Diagnostic Costs: Because a new specialist cannot access a scan or report performed just 24 hours ago at a different clinic, patients are forced to repeat identical investigations (like X-Rays), wasting money and receiving redundant radiation.
  • Cashless Pre-Authorization Delays: Cashless insurance clearance takes up to 8 hours at hospital billing desks because staff must manually collect, print, and match scattered paper records with complex policy clauses.

Slide 3: The Proposed Solution (AyuSync AI)

Active Care Orchestration Network: Moving Beyond Passive Storage

AyuSync AI is not a passive document locker (like DigiLocker, Google Drive, or basic ABHA portals) that simply holds static PDFs. It is an active orchestration engine that reads, structures, and utilizes medical data to solve real clinical, referral, and insurance bottlenecks for patients, doctors, and hospital administrators.

  • Unified Local UID Database (Inter-Hospital Portability): Patients register in seconds and receive a unique local ID (e.g., CARE-928104). Any clinic on the network can immediately retrieve their entire medical history, eliminating paper folders and oral guesswork.
  • Opt-In National Sync: Single-click optional linking to the government ABHA ID to sync records without signup blockers.
  • Print-Ready Visit Summary Reports: Instead of screen-based summaries, AyuSync compiles cluttered files into a concise, printable Visit Summary Report (with a page length that scales dynamically depending on the complexity of the patient's case) that patients print and hand directly to the doctor during the 3-minute consult.
  • Automatic Referral Case Forwarding: If a clinic refers a patient in an emergency, the system automatically dispatches their complete parsed history file to the receiving hospital (e.g. AIIMS) in advance.
  • Automated Insurance & Care Agents (Claims & Free Tests): AyuSync actively eliminates manual billing paperwork and phone calls. The agent automatically compiles clinical files for big-ticket surgery claims and emails them to the TPA (CC'ing the patient), while also monitoring policy terms to automatically schedule free annual health checkups (booking home-sample collections via email) when test dates approach.

Slide 4: Key Features (Dual Dashboard Portals)

Synchronized Portals for Hospitals and Patients

AyuSync connects hospital administrators, doctors, and patients through a single database, keeping everyone on the same page.

  • Hospital Administration Dashboard (Staff-Managed):
  • UID-Driven Record Ingestion: Staff handle all patient records under the local UID, uploading files and registering diagnoses (no computer typing needed from busy doctors).
  • Inter-Hospital Portability: If a patient changes clinics, the new hospital simply enters their UID to instantly view their entire medical journey.
  • Automatic Emergency Referrals: If a patient is transferred to a higher-tier hospital, staff trigger a referral. The system automatically forwards the patient's parsed history file to the receiving hospital (e.g. AIIMS) in advance so it arrives before the patient does.
  • Patient Personal Dashboard (Web/Mobile App):
  • Visual Timeline: Clear chronological map of your health history.
  • Google Drive-Style Vault: Direct upload for past reports, keeping them organized for the future.
  • Shared Claim Tracker: Tracks active pre-authorization clearance states in real-time, matching exactly what the hospital billing console displays.

Slide 5: Key Features (The Clinical AI Engine)

The Print-Ready "Visit Summary Report"

Busy doctors will not read raw files on a computer screen during a 3-minute consultation. AyuSync solves this by converting scattered medical records (for example, consolidating 10 cluttered PDF files into a single, clean 4-Page Visit Summary Report depending on the patient's case complexity) that the patient can print and hand to the doctor.

  • On-Demand Brief Generation: Compile a tailored summary specifically designed for your upcoming doctor visit (e.g., a cardiologist-focused brief prioritizes heart files and medicines).
  • Example Summary Format (e.g., Replaces 10+ Cluttered Files with 4 Pages):
  • Page 1: Chronological medical timeline & active diagnoses.
  • Page 2: Prescription history & active medications.
  • Page 3: Life-threatening warnings (Allergy mismatches, treatment gaps).
  • Page 4: Visual lab biomarker trend charts (e.g., HbA1c curves).
  • Visual Citation Mapping: Every statement in the printable brief features a citation code linking directly to the source PDF and page number, enabling instant clinical validation.

Slide 6: Key Features (Automated Claims & Care Agent)

No More Phone Calls or Paperwork Loops

AyuSync uses intelligent automation to handle time-consuming insurance tasks behind the scenes—both for major surgeries and routine health checkups.

  • Automated Surgery Claims (Big-Ticket Pre-Authorization):
  • The Friction: When a patient is advised for surgery (e.g., Knee Replacement), hospital staff and patients waste hours manually compiling folders of X-Rays, bills, and diagnostics for claim approval.
  • AyuSync Action: The AI automatically reviews the policy terms, compiles all required records (scans, reports, and estimates) into a single packet, and emails it directly to the insurer, CC'ing the patient.
  • Proactive Preventive Care (Free Annual Checkups):
  • The Friction: Most health insurance policies grant 2–3 free health checkups per year, but patients forget them or must call insurance agents repeatedly to book a collection slot.
  • AyuSync Action: The AI automatically scans policy benefits and tracks past test dates. When a test is due, it emails the diagnostic partner directly to book a home sample collection, removing manual coordination entirely.

Slide 7: Technical Architecture & Tech Stack

A Scalable, Cloud-First, Zero-Cost Foundation

AyuSync is built using lightweight, free-tier developer tools so that the MVP runs entirely free of cost during the hackathon.

  • Frontend: React.js + Tailwind CSS (for premium UI layout) + Recharts (for biomarker graphs).
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python) - a super-fast, asynchronous web framework.
  • Database: PostgreSQL on Supabase Cloud - stores structured clinical data.
  • AI Engine: Google Gemini API (Gemini 1.5 Flash for rapid, free image/PDF reading).
  • Email Client: Resend API (for sending claims and lab booking emails).
  • Deployment: Vercel (Frontend) + Render (Backend API), synced to GitHub.

Slide 9: Project Roadmap & Future Vision

A AyuSync Roadmap: Scaling from MVP to National Middleware

AyuSync is designed to evolve from a local, UID-driven clinical assistant into a nationwide digital health pipeline. Our roadmap structures this growth across four strategic integration phases.

  • Phase 1: Foundation & Local Network (The Core MVP)
  • Objective: Establish the local UID database, secure document ingestion (FastAPI + Gemini), and basic email-based claims/preventive workflows.
  • Technology: React.js, FastAPI, local PostgreSQL, and Gemini API for unstructured clinical OCR.
  • Phase 2: National Grid Integration (ABDM & NHCX)
  • Objective: Move from mocked gateways to live integrations with official government health sandboxes.
  • Technology: ABDM Consent Manager APIs (HIU/HIP roles) for federated data access, and the National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX) gateway to automate cashless pre-authorization via official channels.
  • Phase 3: Omnichannel Ingestion & Smart Parsing
  • Objective: Meet patients where they are (WhatsApp) and solve the problem of illegible physician handwriting.
  • Technology: Twilio-backed WhatsApp bot for instant chat-based uploads, and customized transformer-based NLP models trained on local Indian pharmaceutical databases for medical text normalization.
  • Phase 4: Clinical AI Support & Insurer Auto-Adjudication
  • Objective: Transition AyuSync into an active clinical adviser and instant claim settlement node.
  • Technology: Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) for rural clinics, and direct API handshakes with major private and public insurers (TPAs) to settle claims in minutes without manual email reviews.
